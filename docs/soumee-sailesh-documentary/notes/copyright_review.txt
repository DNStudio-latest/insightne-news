2. ASSESSMENT
   - Indian Copyright Act, 1957, s.52(1)(a)(iii) and s.52(1)(b) allow fair dealing for
     reporting current events. Short excerpts used to report on a local singer's credit,
     with attribution, are the kind of use these provisions cover, but the exception is
     decided case by case. It is NOT automatic, and platforms (YouTube Content ID,
     Facebook Rights Manager) may still claim, block or demonetise the videos.
   - The footage is never presented as InsightNE's, never altered to suggest that any
     actor endorses Soumee, and the story is about her credit, not the film's promotion.
   - Before publishing, please either (a) get written permission from Salman Khan Films /
     the music label (Soumee or the music team may be able to help), or (b) have the
     editor approve the fair-dealing use. If neither is possible, see section 4.
