EDITORIAL NOTES: Soumee Sailesh documentary (InsightNE)
Prepared 22 November 2026

1. ITEMS THAT NEED AN EDITOR'S DECISION BEFORE PUBLICATION

1.1 Singers credit differs from the brief
    The brief lists Javed Ali as the male playback singer. The official song video
    (supplied clip, about 36-40 s) credits three singers: JAVED ALI, MOHD DANISH,
    SOUMEE SAILESH. All three videos follow the official credit ("Singers: Javed Ali,
    Mohd Danish, Soumee Sailesh", marked "as credited in the official song video").
    The narration says the official video names these three. Nothing claims she is
    the lead or only singer.

1.2 College name
    The brief says "S.D. Barman College". The college's official name appears to be
    Sachin Deb Barman Memorial Government Music College, Agartala (sdmgovtmusiccollege.in).
    The video keeps the brief's wording ("S.D. Barman College") and the card adds only
    "Tripura". Please confirm the full name with Soumee and change the card if needed.
    Listings also differ on whether the degree is called B.Mus or B.A. (Music); the
    video uses "Bachelor of Music (B.Mus)" as supplied.

1.3 Constituency
    The brief gives "Kalyanpur". The assembly constituency is officially named
    Kalyanpur-Pramodenagar. Because this could not be confirmed for Chebri, the
    constituency is NOT mentioned in the videos.

1.4 "Released"
    In the interview Soumee says the song "has been released" in the film. This is in
    her words (captioned). The narration does not state a release date or the film's
    release status.

1.5 Interview translation
    The interview is in Bengali (not Hindi). The transcript was made with Whisper
    large-v3 and corrected by hand; three places are marked [check] in
    02_TRANSCRIPTS/interview_transcript.txt (Khowai phrase in B1, "patience" vs
    "passion" in B5, end of B7). A Bengali-speaking editor should confirm the English
    captions against the recording.

1.6 What is NOT in her interview
    The interview does not mention A.R. Rahman, her mother, music genres, a five-year
    goal, advice to young singers, or working with Himesh Reshammiya. These points come
    from the brief and appear only as attributed narration and plain info cards (no
    quotation marks). Quotation graphics use only her actual (translated) words.

1.7 Which vocal is hers
    The song clips do not let us isolate Soumee's voice from the other singers. The
    song excerpts are labelled as the song, not as "her voice".

2. FACTS ADDED BEYOND THE BRIEF (all checked)
    - Chebri is a few kilometres from Khowai town (about 11 km: villageinfo.in /
      Census 2011 village code 271915).
    - Khowai district was carved out of West Tripura in January 2012; its northern edge
      is the international border with Bangladesh (khowai.nic.in; Wikipedia).
    - Sachin Dev Burman (1906-1975) belonged to Tripura's royal family; his son Rahul Dev
      Burman (1939-1994) became a major name in Hindi film music.
    - Straight-line distance Chebri/Khowai to Mumbai: 2,012 km great-circle from Khowai
      town (24.065 N, 91.605 E) to Mumbai (19.076 N, 72.878 E); Chebri is about 11 km
      away, so it is shown as "about 2,000 km", labelled as aerial, not road or travel
      distance.
    - A narration line explains playback singing, so viewers do not take the film's
      actors in the song footage for Soumee.
