## Rules for future Reels
1. Post the vertical file to Instagram, YouTube Shorts and Facebook Reels. Keep 16:9 for YouTube and Facebook feed only.
2. Her face and voice in the first 3 seconds (the vertical already does this at 0:03).
3. No still or text-only shot longer than 3 seconds. Maps 3-4 s, credits card 3 s or move the credits to the caption.
4. Target 30-35 s for Reels. The 53 s vertical is still long.
5. Song footage: 3-4 s clip at most, and use her own interview audio as the main sound. Copyright claims can mute the audio or limit reach.
6. End with a question or share prompt (for example "Share this with someone from Khowai"), not only a logo.
7. Credits go in the caption and pinned comment, not on a long full-screen card.
8. Pinned comment inviting people from Tripura to reply.
9. One idea per card; at most two short lines of text on a phone screen.
10. The AI narration should be paced with pauses and emphasis, but the script may only say what is verified.

## Do not copy from the review
