**Description**
Soumee Sailesh, a singer from Chebri in Tripura's Khowai district, has sung playback on "Madirapan", a song from the Salman Khan-starrer Maatrubhumi. The music is by Himesh Reshammiya and the lyrics are by Sameer Anjaan. The official song video credits Javed Ali, Mohd Danish and Soumee Sailesh as its singers.

Soumee, also known as Sanhita Debnath, learnt music first from her mother, trained in classical music and earned a Bachelor of Music degree from S.D. Barman College. She moved to Mumbai in 2017 and has worked on television series, music albums, films, advertisements and live shows.

In this InsightNE documentary, she speaks about her journey from Tripura to Mumbai, her dreams and her message to young singers.
