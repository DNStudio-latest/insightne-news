AI text checker and style editor

Estimates whether a text reads as AI-generated, shows the words and sentences behind the score, and suggests plainer wording. Your text stays in your browser. Nothing is uploaded.

Read before use. A score is a statistical estimate, not proof of who wrote a text. The built-in model was right on about 9 in 10 test texts, but it wrongly flagged about 1 in 13 human texts, and it is less accurate on newer AI models and on unfamiliar genres. Formal academic prose and writing by non-native English speakers can be flagged in error. Do not use a score alone to accuse anyone. The tool is designed for English text.
